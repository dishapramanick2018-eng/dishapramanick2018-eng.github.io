# Selected Analytics Projects

## ⭐ E-commerce Product Conversion & Growth Analytics

A product analytics case study focused on diagnosing conversion deterioration across 30,000 shopping sessions.

Rather than stopping at topline conversion trends, the analysis investigates the funnel across device, acquisition channel, customer type, category, and device-channel interactions to identify where performance changed relative to historical baselines.

### Key Findings

- 30,000 shopping sessions analyzed
- Overall conversion rate: 18.27%
- Identified Mobile × Paid Search as the key deteriorating segment
- Conversion declined from 17.65% to 9.40% in the diagnostic period
- Primary friction isolated at Checkout → Purchase
- Estimated purchase gap: 98 orders under the historical-baseline scenario

### Tools

`Python` `SQL` `Excel` `Streamlit` `Pandas` `Plotly`  
`Product Analytics` `Funnel Analysis` `Root Cause Analysis`
